MASTER VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of July 25, 2026, by and between Tata Group ("Client") and Global Tech Solutions Inc. ("Vendor").

1. SERVICES PROVIDED
Vendor agrees to provide enterprise software support and cloud infrastructure maintenance as detailed in the attached Statement of Work (SOW). Vendor shall be the exclusive provider of these services to the Client for the duration of this Agreement.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within 30 days of receipt. Late payments shall incur a penalty of 5% per month. 

3. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of proprietary information. The obligations of confidentiality shall survive for a period of ten (10) years following the termination of this Agreement.

4. INDEMNIFICATION
Vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Client from any and all claims, damages, or expenses arising from Vendor's breach. Furthermore, Client agrees to fully indemnify and hold Vendor harmless against any and all third-party claims, without any cap or limitation on the monetary amount.

5. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
In no event shall Vendor be liable for any lost profits, loss of data, or indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. However, Client's total liability under this Agreement shall remain entirely unlimited.

6. TERMINATION
This Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial term of five (5) years. Client may not terminate this Agreement for convenience during the initial term. 

7. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, USA. Any disputes shall be exclusively resolved in the federal courts located in Manhattan, New York.
