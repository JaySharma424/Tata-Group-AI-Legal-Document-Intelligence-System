# Tata AI Legal Intelligence System - Demo Walkthrough Script

**Role:** Legal Compliance Specialist / Enterprise Auditor  
**Platform:** Tata AI Legal Intelligence Portal  

---

## 🎯 Scene 1: Enterprise Governance & Dynamic Key Configuration
1. Log into the **Tata Group AI Legal Intelligence Portal**.
2. Navigate to the **Admin Control Portal** tab.
3. Show the **LLM Provider & Key Management** panel.
4. **Key Feature Demonstration:**
   * Enter a new Gemini API key in the input box.
   * Click **Test Connection** — point out the successful connection response.
   * Click **Save Configuration**.
   * Note that the active key suffix (e.g., `...fkkQ`) updates instantly across the workspace and is persisted permanently in PostgreSQL.

---

## 📄 Scene 2: Document Ingestion & Multimodal OCR Scanning
1. Switch to the **Workspace** tab.
2. Under **Upload Legal Contract**:
   * Select a contract PDF (e.g., `Tata_Test_Vendor_Agreement.pdf`).
   * Choose Business Unit: **Procurement**.
   * Category: **Vendor Agreement**.
   * Priority: **High**.
3. Click **Analyze Document**.
4. Observe the live KPI cards populate:
   * **OCR Extraction Quality:** ~96%
   * **Pages Processed:** 1
   * **Entities Detected:** 87 NLP entities

---

## 🧠 Scene 3: LangGraph Pipeline & RAG Risk Matrix
1. Click on the **Clauses & RAG Risk Matrix** sub-tab.
2. Review the extracted clauses generated by the LangGraph pipeline:
   * Point out the **Risk Level Badge** (e.g., `HIGH Risk`, `MEDIUM Risk`).
   * Show the **Tata AI Policy Grounding & Rationale**.
   * Highlight the **Cited RAG Knowledge Base Reference** (e.g., `TAX-1`), proving that the reasoning is grounded in Qdrant policy vectors.

---

## 📊 Scene 4: AI RAGAS Scorecard Verification
1. Click on the **AI RAGAS Scorecard** sub-tab.
2. Point out the top metadata banner:
   * **Engine:** `gemini-3.5-flash`
   * **Active Key:** `...fkkQ`
3. Highlight the 4 live RAG evaluation metrics:
   * **Faithfulness:** Confirms zero hallucinations.
   * **Answer Relevancy:** Confirms the reasoning directly answers the clause prompt.
   * **Context Precision:** Measures Qdrant vector retrieval accuracy.
   * **Context Recall:** Measures policy coverage against compliance rules.

## ✍️ Scene 5: Human-in-the-Loop Sign-Off & PDF Export
1. Scroll down to **Human-in-the-Loop Governance Sign-Off**.
2. Enter reviewer notes: *"Approved under Procurement Exception Ex-2026 after legal review."*
3. Click **Accept Contract**. Show the green confirmation badge.
4. Click **Export Audit PDF**.
